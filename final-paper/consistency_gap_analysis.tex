The consistency gap reveals that:
\begin{itemize}
    \item The gap is \emph{larger} for stronger models in absolute terms, because their higher IA creates more room for SCR to lag behind. However, the gap \emph{ratio} (SCR/IA) tends to be more favorable for stronger models.
    \item \method{} reduces the gap by improving SCR more than it changes IA, confirming that our method specifically targets the consistency failure mode rather than broadly improving reasoning.
\end{itemize}
